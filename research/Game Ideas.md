# Game Ideas

Each idea mixes two things the data says are working (see [[Top Roblox Games - Nov 2026]] and [[TikTok Notes]]). Test fast before committing: bare-bones version + one cheap ad each.

## 1. Sneak & Steal: Giant's House
Sneak into a sleeping giant's house, grab rare loot, carry it back to your base. Other players can raid your base.
- Mixes: steal/collect loop (Steal An Egg #1, Break and Steal an Egg 97% liked) + quiet stealth (DONT SPLASH, 9.7M visits/day)
- Twist: noise wakes the giant, so it's PvE and PvP at once
- Upside: highest

## 2. Grow a Monster ← building now
Throw food at your pet monster until it's huge. Biggest on the server wins.
- Mixes: silly throwing (Throw Food For Animals, top 5) + grow/collect (Grow a Garden)
- Money: rare foods, mutations, 2x growth
- Safest bet, easiest to build → `games/grow-a-monster`

## 3. Blind Driver
One player drives with a near-black screen; the partner sees the map and calls turns.
- Based on: Backseat Rally (92% liked, 2.1M visits/day at 28 days): co-op dependency + yelling with friends spreads it
- Smallest version: one track, two seats, a timer
- Most original, cheapest to test

## 4. Ragdoll Delivery
Launch yourself as a ragdoll across a city to deliver packages. Unlock bigger cannons and crazier routes.
- Based on: Hyper Realistic Ragdoll (+245% in a day, top 15); fails make shareable clips

## 5. Ice Cream Truck: Night Route
Sell ice cream around the neighborhood by day; after dark the customers aren't human. Co-op.
- Based on: job-by-day, horror-by-night (Sell Fries!, 2.25M visits/day) + co-op survival (99 Nights)

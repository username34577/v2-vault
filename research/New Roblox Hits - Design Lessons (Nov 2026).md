## Patterns across the newer hits (hypotheses)

1. **The verb is physical and can fail.** Splash = caught. Grip runs out = fall. Pushed out = lose. Breaking eggs while guards chase you. Every one of these has a way to mess up that you can *see* and laugh at. Our THROW button has none.
2. **Short runs with a clear exit.** DONT SPLASH stages, Spider Arm's 13 stages, Sell Fries' "find the key". Each run takes minutes and ends with a result screen. That gives "one more go".
3. **Risk vs. reward you choose yourself.** Steal-and-run games: carry the loot home or get caught. Carry more, earn more, risk more.
4. **Readable threat.** A monster or guard whose rules you learn (it hears sound, it patrols). Learning the rule *is* the skill.
5. **Upgrades change how you play, not just numbers.** More spider arms = new routes; a better pickaxe = bigger eggs.
6. **Made fast, launched early, updated.** Cat Battles took 3 days. Scope one strong loop, ship it, iterate.
7. **Creature care plus stealth works** (Woodlands): feed creatures + sneak past danger + bring things home. Closest to our theme.

## What to take for Grow a Monster (and what not)

- **Take:** a forage run with a learnable threat, carry-home risk, upgrades that open routes, short runs with a result moment.
- **Don't copy:** stealing from other players' bases (Steal-a-X is crowded and draws griefing complaints), horror tone (our style is cozy-goofy), ring-out PvP.
- **Our angle:** your **own monster** is the reason you go out and the thing that grows. It should be a companion you care about, not a stat.

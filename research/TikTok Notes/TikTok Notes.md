## Top takeaways
1. **Fix the first 30 seconds.** Players should get it with no tutorial; don't lock them out during onboarding. Set up funnel analytics to see where they leave. → [[funnels-and-tutorial-lockout]], [[five-game-improvements]], [[prompt-funnel-analytics]]
2. **Make every action feel good.** Sound, animation, visual feedback on buys/upgrades/tasks. → [[prompt-visual-feedback]], [[prompt-nintendo-game-feel]]
3. **Shop basics:** a cheap first item (<10 Robux), and when players can't afford something, prompt the currency pack right away. → [[monetization-crash-course]]
4. **Ads:** start with 3–5 honest thumbnails, cut weak ones fast, judge by cost per play, not just CTR. → [[roblox-ads-and-thumbnails]]
5. **Your vault is the edge.** Put your game's art style and standards in it, organized with master notes Claude reads first. → [[obsidian-vault-for-ai]], [[obsidian-agent-structure]]
6. **Test ideas fast** before sinking weeks into one. Remix proven mechanics; don't clone games. → [[ship-many-small-games]], [[dead-game-method]]

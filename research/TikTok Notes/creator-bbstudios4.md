## Takeaways
- **The quality bar.** This is what Claude-built Roblox UI, VFX, cutscenes and models can look like with the right setup: themed, layered, animated. Compare your own builds against it.
- **UI ideas worth stealing as techniques (not art):** panels that fade in row by row; an "OWNED" state on bought passes; flying the bought item to the player; a 3D rotating inspect view for weapons; decorations around the shop frame that match the event theme.
- **A lot of it is other people's IP:** Persona 5's menu, Yu-Gi-Oh! gods, a Dragon Ball-style dragon, JoJo, a Porsche, and "import other games into Roblox". Fine for a TikTok flex; in a published game it's the kind of thing that gets taken down. The vault's own rule applies: original art and names only.
- **Marketing format:** no-talk clip + bold "POV: …" or "Claude can now…" caption + trending slowed audio = hundreds of thousands of views. A cheap format for promoting your own game.
- He sells lessons and game promotion by DM, so the free method isn't on TikTok.

## Worth it?
Low for learning how. Medium as a reference for what "good" looks like and for UI-juice ideas. Skip anything that copies someone else's game or characters.

# Creator: @rxz_dev (rxz)

**Bio:** "Visit my website and join the discord for all my resources · rxz.info" · ~200 followers · 3 videos (Nov 2026) · Back to [[TikTok Notes]]

**What he does:** gives away Claude skills for "anti-slop" Roblox games. The skills themselves are behind his Discord ("comment anti slop"). His site rxz.info also lists a one-line-to-thumbnail tool and an AI video editor. None of the skill files are public, so the vault can't copy them, but it already has skills covering every one.

## Video 1: Fix AI slop games in 3 steps (32K)
[Watch](https://www.tiktok.com/@rxz_dev/video/7695403687035931912)
1. **Use skills, not bare prompts.** E.g. a Roblox UI skill instead of "make me a shop menu"; it gives the AI a proven framework.
2. **Memory file.** Have it keep a file of your colours, fonts and how the game should feel, so it doesn't reinvent them each time.
3. **Master prompt.** Have another chatbot turn your idea into a better prompt, then paste it in. (Same as [[ai-master-prompt-for-maps]].)

> "Slop isn't the AI being bad. It's the AI guessing because nobody told it what you wanted."

## Video 2: Fix AI slop maps with 3 skills (1.8K)
[Watch](https://www.tiktok.com/@rxz_dev/video/7704276098045054214)
1. **3D model skill:** better-looking models.
2. **Map skill:** makes sure parts are reachable by players, fills gaps, hides signs of AI.
3. **Texture skill:** "smallest of the three but most impact"; players spot AI by the textures first.

## Video 3: Fix AI slop animations with 3 skills (85K)
[Watch](https://www.tiktok.com/@rxz_dev/video/7704695861770538258)
1. **Animation skill:** animates in Blender instead of the default Roblox animator, for more complex animation.
2. **SFX skill:** searches thousands of sound assets and wires them into your animations.
3. **Cutscene skill:** turns plain interactions into cinematic moments.

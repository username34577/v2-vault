# Add 'earn while offline'

**Creator:** @buildchamp54 · **Length:** 25s · [Watch on TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@buildchamp54/video/7634236011404446990)
**Theme:** Game design · Back to [[TikTok Notes]]

## Summary
Offline earnings boost retention and give players a pile of cash when they come back.

## Key points
- 1. Save a timestamp when the player leaves (DataStore).
- 2. On rejoin, compute seconds offline.
- 3. Multiply by the player's income rate.
- 4. Show a popup with how much they earned.

## Worth it?
Concrete and simple to build. Cap the max offline time so it can't be abused.

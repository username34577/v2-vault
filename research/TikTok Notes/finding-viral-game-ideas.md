# Finding game ideas (copy web/Steam concepts)

**Creator:** @jacobunfiltered · **Length:** 42s · [Watch on TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@jacobunfiltered/video/7701174600675249439)
**Theme:** Ideas / market · Back to [[TikTok Notes]]

## Summary
Suggests copying ideas from free web-game sites (like Poki) and concepts people post on Twitter before they hit Steam.

## Key points
- Browse free browser-game sites for proven simple concepts.
- Watch Twitter for new indie concepts.

## Worth it?
Using proven mechanics is normal. Copying someone's specific game or unreleased idea is not cool and can get reported. Remix, don't clone.

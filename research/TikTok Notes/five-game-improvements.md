# 5 ways to improve your game

**Creator:** @momentumcash · **Length:** 31s · [Watch on TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@momentumcash/video/7704805358753139999)
**Theme:** Game design · Back to [[TikTok Notes]]

## Summary
Quick checklist.

## Key points
- 1. First 30 seconds: players should know what to do with no tutorial.
- 2. Rewarding progression: a clear reason to keep playing.
- 3. Cut downtime and waiting.
- 4. More social: reasons to interact, compete, or play with friends.
- 5. Make important actions feel satisfying (sound, animation, visual feedback).

Good checklist to run on every build.

# Getting out of 16+: the Kids and Select gate

**Creator:** @oksitaine · [Video 1: why is my game 16+](https://www.tiktok.com/@oksitaine/video/7709473520237022486) (171s, 25K plays) · [Video 2: 5 things before your first game](https://www.tiktok.com/@oksitaine/video/7707440528274640150) (90s, 109K plays) · Back to [[TikTok Notes]]

## What he says
- Every new game starts as a trial: only age-checked 16+ players can play it. Not a bug or a ban.
- To open it to all ages: (1) ID verification + 2-step verification, (2) the **full** content questionnaire, (3) 1,000 Robux refundable fee, or nothing if you've had Roblox Plus/Premium for 2 months, (4) enough **highly engaged players** (HEP) in 60 days.
- A HEP = an account with real history, real time in your game, and a purchase **anywhere** on Roblox in the last 60 days. Only verified 16+ players count.
- Roblox sells a shortcut (50,000 Robux, locked 90 days). He never used it.
- Use Ads Manager with the objective made for this (he calls it "Plays, qualify for Kids and Select"; it used to be called Engagement). Higher cost per play is expected. ~$20/day, up to 10 thumbnails.
- His cost: $177 (game 1, Oct 24 → Nov 5), ~$100 (game 2); others told him ~$60. He thinks hot title words like "egg" or "steal" raise ad cost.
- Threshold history he gives: 500 → 250 (August) → 100 (November).
- Progress is in Creator Hub → publish settings → Audience Reach; updates hourly.

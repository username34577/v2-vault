# Monetization crash course

**Creator:** @palm_dev · **Length:** 62s · [Watch on TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@palm_dev/video/7698917348379561247)
**Theme:** Monetization · Back to [[TikTok Notes]]

## Summary
What a good shop needs.

## Key points
- A shop that looks good: starter packs, a 'best value' tag, clean icons, shine on buttons.
- One very cheap item (under 10 Robux) to get players to make a first purchase.
- When a player clicks something they can't afford, immediately prompt the smallest currency pack that covers it, then auto-buy the item after.
- Make paid items visible in the world or on screen, not only behind a shop button.

## Worth it?
Practical. The 'can't afford → prompt purchase' trick is a big one.

## Checked
- **Mirror's Edge example: Partially Verified.** The idea is right; the colour is wrong. He says it used white or yellow. Runner Vision highlights the path in **red** against a mostly white world (EA, reviews). The principle holds: one reserved colour = "go here".
- **Verified** as consistent: everything else matches the vault's onboarding notes and the `lihfolk-tutorial` skill (8-word objective text, show-then-do, contextual tips later). Nothing contradicts the vault.
- **Unverified (design idea):** the **loaner item** (strong temporary gear that breaks, then an upsell). It's a design idea, not a verified result. Keep it fair: no pressure on young players. Check [[Marketing to Kids and Teens - Age Rules and Paid-Promo Disclosure]] and the monetization compliance skill before shipping it.

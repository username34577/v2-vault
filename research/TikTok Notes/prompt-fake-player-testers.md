# Prompt: sub-agents as fake players

**Creator:** @oddblockstudios · **Length:** 16s · [Watch on TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@oddblockstudios/video/7709719316576505102)
**Theme:** Claude prompts · Back to [[TikTok Notes]]

## Summary
Have Claude spin up sub-agents that act as different player types and try to break the game.

## Key points
- Player types: speedrunner skipping everything, hacker trying to get free shop items, little kid mashing every button, player who quits the second they're bored.
- Each tries to break the game; then fix everything they find before real players do.

# Running Roblox ads (9% CTR breakdown)

**Creator:** @duomillionz · **Length:** 111s · [Watch on TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@duomillionz/video/7709034742859042062)
**Theme:** Ads / marketing · Back to [[TikTok Notes]]

## Summary
How he tests ad creatives. Says he hit 9% CTR across millions of impressions.

## Key points
- Start with 3–5 different thumbnails that all honestly show your game (no clickbait).
- Broad targeting early. About 15–20 ad credits a day.
- Cut a creative under 3% CTR, or one with 5,000 impressions and barely any spend.
- Take the best ones and make 3–5 variants of each.
- High CTR isn't everything: compare cost per play. One 5.4% CTR ad cost 5x more per play than a 3.5% one.
- For a 'highly engaged players' boost: 3 active creatives, ~10K impressions each, 5%+ CTR, 15+ clicks.

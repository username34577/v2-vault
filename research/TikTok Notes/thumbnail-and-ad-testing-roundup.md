## Checked
- **Creatives per campaign:** the vault (from Roblox docs) says **up to 10**, distributed evenly. @ramszn0's "25" doesn't match that. He may be running several campaigns, or the limit has changed. **Unverified** (check in Ads Manager).
- **"Roblox spends more on the winners":** the vault says creatives are distributed evenly and you toggle losers off. His screenshot suggests uneven spend. **Unverified:** watch per-creative spend yourself.
- **ForgeGUI: Verified** (the product, free tier and Studio plugin are on its own site; the MCP plugin is **Partially Verified**, from a third-party write-up only). Thumbnails, icons and GUI from prompts or reference images, free tier plus paid credits, a Studio plugin, and an MCP plugin (per a third-party write-up). Paid credits burn fast with heavy iteration.
- **qptr.io: Verified** (DevForum thread). Previews your thumbnail next to others the way players see it (DevForum, Jul 2026). It does not measure real qPTR; Roblox's thumbnail personalization does.
- **"Most top games use AI thumbnails":** **Unverified.** Related fact: a Nov 2026 report says Roblox states 80%+ of its top 100 experiences use its AI creation tools.

## Takeaway (combined)
Test **different concepts**, not colour swaps (valeria + vault). Judge by **cost per play and the D1 of the players each creative brings**, not CTR alone (vault + @duomillionz). Have funnels set up before spending (captainamado). A cheap small test (~$5–20/day for a few days) is enough to decide whether to keep going.

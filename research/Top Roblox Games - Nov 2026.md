## Patterns (what's working)

1. **Steal / collect loops are king right now.** Steal a Brainrot → Steal An Egg → Break and Steal an Egg / Fake an Egg. Simple loop: get item → protect it in your base → steal other players' stuff. Players mess with each other, so it's social.
2. **Simple idea you can explain in one sentence.** "Throw food at animals." "Don't splash." "Cats sumo wrestling." The name alone tells you the game.
3. **Horror is still hot**, especially co-op and "normal job that turns scary" (99 Nights, DONT SPLASH, Sell Fries!).
4. **Small teams can win fast.** Several top-10 growers are under 2 months old. You don't need years on the platform.
5. **Old giants survive on constant updates and events** (Adopt Me, Blox Fruits, Brookhaven). Live updates keep players coming back.
6. **High like rating matters.** Most winners sit at 85–97% liked.

## Takeaways for my game

- Copying "Steal An ___" directly is crowded now. Better: take a **proven loop** (collect + protect + steal, or job-then-horror) and put a **fresh, funny theme** on it.
- Keep the first version **tiny**: one map, one core loop, done in weeks, not months.
- Plan for **updates** from day one (new items/events) since that's what keeps games alive.
- A good first practice build is still an **obby**. Then a small collect / steal prototype.
